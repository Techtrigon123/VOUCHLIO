# SEO for vouchlio.com

What is built into the site, what you need to do by hand, and a plan for earning backlinks.

## 1. Before launch: settings

| Setting | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL=https://vouchlio.com` | Hosting environment variables | Every canonical tag, Open Graph URL, robots.txt and sitemap URL is built from it. If it is wrong, Google is pointed at the wrong site. |
| `NEXT_PUBLIC_GOOGLE_SITE_VERIFICATION` | Hosting environment variables | Search Console verification (section 3). |
| `social` profile URLs | `lib/site.ts` | Shown in the footer and in the Organization schema (`sameAs`). Empty ones stay hidden. |
| Legal entity, address, grievance officer | `lib/site.ts` | Shown on the legal pages; a real business identity also builds trust with Google. |

## 2. What the site already does

- **robots.txt** (`app/robots.ts`): allows public pages; blocks `/dashboard`, `/setup`, password-reset pages and `/api/`.
- **sitemap.xml** (`app/sitemap.ts`): the 15 public pages. Add new public pages to the `PAGES` list.
- **Canonical, title, description, Open Graph and Twitter tags** on every public page through `pageMeta()` in `lib/seo.ts`. Use it for every new public page.
- **noindex** only on private or one-off pages: dashboard, setup, forgot and reset password.
- **Schema markup** on the home page: Organization, WebSite, SoftwareApplication (free plan) and FAQPage. The FAQ comes from `components/marketing/faq-data.ts`, so the page and the schema always match.
- **Open Graph image** generated by `app/opengraph-image.tsx` (1200×630).
- **404 page** (`app/not-found.tsx`) returns a real 404 status.
- **HTTPS**: `proxy.ts` redirects http to https in production, and HSTS (`next.config.ts`) keeps browsers on https.
- **Internal links**: every tool page links to the other tools, the sign-up page and pricing; the footer links to the main tools.

## 3. Verify Google Search Console

1. Go to <https://search.google.com/search-console> and click **Add property**.
2. Choose **Domain** (`vouchlio.com`) if you can edit DNS. It covers http, https, www and every subdomain. Add the TXT record it shows at your domain registrar and click **Verify**. *Or* choose **URL prefix** (`https://vouchlio.com`) and the **HTML tag** method: copy only the `content="…"` value into `NEXT_PUBLIC_GOOGLE_SITE_VERIFICATION`, redeploy, then click **Verify**.
3. Open **Sitemaps** and submit `sitemap.xml`.
4. Open **URL inspection**, enter the home page and each tool page, and click **Request indexing**.
5. Also add the site to **Bing Webmaster Tools** (<https://www.bing.com/webmasters>). It can import the property straight from Search Console.
6. After 2–4 weeks, check **Pages** (indexing problems), **Core Web Vitals** and **Performance** (the searches you appear for).

## 4. Backlink strategy

Vouchlio sells to a narrow audience: Indian travel agents, tour operators and DMCs. A few links from sites they read are worth far more than hundreds of generic links. **Never buy links or use link farms or private blog networks.** Google penalises them, and recovering takes months.

### Month 1: foundations (easy, permanent links)

- **Google Business Profile** for the registered office, with the website link.
- **SaaS directories**: Capterra, G2, GetApp, Software Advice, SaaSworthy, Product Hunt and AlternativeTo (list Vouchlio as an alternative to the tools agents use today). Fill each profile completely with screenshots.
- **Indian business listings**: IndiaMART, JustDial, Sulekha, Startup India (if registered) and the Tracxn/Crunchbase company profile.
- **Your own profiles**: LinkedIn company page, YouTube channel and Instagram. Add each to `lib/site.ts`.

### Months 1–3: the travel trade (highest-value links)

- **Associations**: TAAI, TAFI, IATO, ADTOI and state tour-operator bodies. Ask about member-benefit or technology-partner listings, or sponsor a chapter event in return for a link.
- **Travel trade media**: TravTalk India, Travel Trends Today, TravelBiz Monitor, Trade Journal Online, Express TravelWorld and BW Hotelier. Pitch stories they can use, such as "how small agencies make GST invoices in two minutes" or launch and funding news, rather than adverts.
- **Partners and integrations**: payment providers, PDF and WhatsApp tools, hotel and B2B booking platforms. Ask for a listing on their partner or integration page, and link back to them.
- **Customer showcase**: ask happy agencies to add a "Documents by Vouchlio" link to their website footer or vouchers in return for a feature in a case study.

### Ongoing: content that earns links

Publish genuinely useful pages that agents and bloggers want to cite. Each one is a reason to link and a page that ranks on its own.

- **Free templates**: hotel voucher, proforma invoice and receipt templates (PDF/Word) linked from the tool pages.
- **Guides**: "GST on tour packages explained (5% vs 18%)", "SAC codes for travel services", "What a hotel voucher must include", "How to write a travel quotation".
- **Data**: a yearly "State of Indian travel agencies" survey of your users. Trade media and bloggers link to original numbers.
- **Free calculators**: a GST-on-travel calculator or a markup and commission calculator.

Then pitch each new resource to the trade media above and to travel-business bloggers.

### Outreach that works

- **Guest articles** for travel-business blogs and the newsletters of travel-agent training institutes (IATA courses, travel and tourism colleges).
- **Unlinked mentions**: set a Google Alert for "Vouchlio". When someone mentions you without a link, politely ask them to add one.
- **Broken-link building**: find dead links on travel-trade resource pages (for example with the Ahrefs free broken-link checker) and suggest your guide as the replacement.
- **Answer questions**: Quora, Reddit (r/travelagents), LinkedIn groups and travel-agent Facebook and WhatsApp communities. Be helpful first and link only when it answers the question. These links are usually nofollow, but they bring real visitors and brand searches.

### Measure monthly

- Search Console **Links** report: new linking sites and the pages they link to.
- Referring domains (Ahrefs Webmaster Tools is free for your own site): aim for 5–10 relevant new domains a month.
- Rankings and clicks for target searches: "hotel voucher generator", "travel agency software India", "GST invoice for travel agents", "proforma invoice for tour package".

## 5. Still worth doing

- **Remove unused images** from `public/` (`voyenta-logo.png` is 1.2 MB, and `voyenta-logo-mark.png`, `brand/vouchlio-logo.png`, `brand/vouchlio-logo-dark.png` and `brand/vouchlio-mark-light.png` are unused). They don't slow pages down, but they bloat the deployment.
- **Core Web Vitals**: after launch, test the home page in PageSpeed Insights (<https://pagespeed.web.dev>) on mobile and check the Core Web Vitals report in Search Console once it has data.
